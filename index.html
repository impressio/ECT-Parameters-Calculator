Total Charge

\[ \text{Total Charge} \, (mC) = \left( \frac{\text{Pulse Width} \, (ms) \times \text{Frequency} \, (Hz) \times 2}{1000} \right) \times \text{Duration} \, (s) \times \text{Current} \, (mA) \]

Energy

\[ \text{Energy} \, (J) = \left( \frac{\text{Total Charge} \, (mC)}{1000} \right) \times \left( \frac{\text{Current} \, (mA)}{1000} \right) \times \text{Impedance} \, (\Omega) \]
